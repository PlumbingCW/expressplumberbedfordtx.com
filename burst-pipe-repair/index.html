Emergency burst pipe repair in Bedford, TX begins with stopping the water, then removing the damaged section, replacing it with new pipe and testing the system. A burst can come from freezing, from corrosion, from high pressure or from a joint that finally gave out.

North Texas freezes are infrequent, so many homes have little pipe insulation. February 2021 showed how many homes across the region were caught off guard when a prolonged freeze split lines in attics, exterior walls and garages. Even a short hard freeze can do the same.

Burst pipe: what to do in the first minutes

Water spraying or pouring from a pipe is an emergency. Every minute adds damage to floors, drywall and belongings. Stop the water, then call the 24/7 line.

Do these in order.

Shut the main valve

Turn the main shutoff off. If you cannot find it, shut the meter valve at the street with a wrench, if you can do so safely.

Open a low faucet

With the main off, open the lowest tap to drain the line and relieve pressure.

Switch off power in wet areas

If water is near outlets or the panel, turn off that circuit from a dry spot. Do not stand in water.

Save what you can

Move furniture and electronics out of the way and photograph the damage for insurance.

Why pipes burst

Freezing

Water expands as it freezes. Pipes in attics, garages and exterior walls are most at risk.

Corrosion and age

Old galvanized steel and worn copper can thin out until they split.

High water pressure

Pressure above a safe range stresses every fitting, and a pressure reducing valve can fail.

Poor joints or defects

Faulty solder or fittings can fail months or years after install.

How the repair works

After shutdown we locate the break and check the pipe on either side for more damage. Short sections are cut out and replaced with matching material, using copper, PEX or other approved pipe. We then restore pressure slowly and check every joint for leaks.

A pipe that splits at one spot may have more weak points. If several sections look worn, we explain whether a larger replacement or repiping would help.

Spot repair

  • Replaces just the failed section.
  • Fastest and lowest cost.
  • Fits a single isolated failure.
  • Weak spots elsewhere remain.

Larger replacement

  • Covers a whole run or zone.
  • Fits repeated failures or old pipe.
  • Higher cost and more work.
  • Reduces the chance of a repeat.

After a freeze

Texas freezes cause many bursts at once, so plumbers book up fast. Know your main shutoff and call early.

Attic and garage lines

Supply pipes in unheated spaces freeze first. Insulation sleeves and covers on outdoor faucets help.

Water damage cleanup

Drying and mold prevention start within a day or two of a flood. A restoration company handles drying, and the plumber handles the pipe.

Pressure check

High street pressure shortens pipe life. We test it after a repair and recommend a pressure reducing valve when needed.

What moves the price

A single accessible split costs far less than a failure inside a wall or slab. A price is given before work starts.

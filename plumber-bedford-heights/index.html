Bedford Heights is a residential part of Bedford, Texas, named in city records for the Bedford Heights Addition. The Hurst-Euless-Bedford school district runs Bedford Heights Elementary at 1000 Cummings Drive and Bedford Junior High at 325 Carolyn Drive, both with 76021 addresses. If you live near those two schools, this page is written for you.

We cannot say where Bedford Heights officially begins and ends, because the sources we found do not draw a boundary. What they do show is a platted area, a pair of schools and a set of named streets. The sections below stick to those facts and to plumbing steps that apply to almost any home.

What the records show about Bedford Heights

A named addition on city lists

The City of Bedford's 2022 street improvement agenda lists a Bedford Heights Addition with Wayne, Harwood Terrace and Shady Brook among its streets. That puts the name in city paperwork and not only on school signs.

Two schools as anchors

Bedford Heights Elementary sits on Cummings Drive and Bedford Junior High sits on Carolyn Drive. Both appear on the district list for Bedford with 76021 addresses.

Street work on the city map

The same 2022 materials label Carolyn Drive, Carolyn Court and Wayside Drive on the city's active projects map. Street work is a good reason to call 811 before any digging near a service line.

Water source

Bedford buys treated water supplied through the Trinity River Authority's Tarrant County Water Supply Project from Lake Arlington and also draws on Trinity Aquifer wells. The city's 2025 report lists total hardness of 110 ppm.

Start with the age of your own house

Housing guides describe Bedford's single-family homes as built mostly between the 1970s and early 2000s. That is a citywide picture, and it tells you nothing certain about your street. The Tarrant Appraisal District record for your address shows the year your house was built, so look that up first.

Age is a reason to inspect, not proof of trouble. A house of any age can have sound pipes, and a newer house can have a failing fitting. What age does tell you is how many times the water heater, the supply valves and the faucets may already have been replaced.

A 15 minute walk-through before you call

  • Find the main water shutoff and turn it once to confirm it moves.
  • Read the date on the water heater label and write it down.
  • Look under every sink for damp wood, stains or a musty smell.
  • Check the base of each toilet for soft flooring or movement.
  • Note any room where a drain gurgles or runs slowly.

Plumbing services homeowners here ask about

Most calls from an established neighborhood start with a tank that stopped heating or a drain that slowed down. Water heater repair covers heating elements, thermostats and leaks at a fitting. Drain cleaning covers sinks, tubs and the main line, and leak detection helps when a bill rises with no visible cause.

Older fixtures are common in homes that have stood for decades, so faucet repair and toilet repair come up often. If a house shows repeated leaks in several rooms, ask about repiping before paying for the same repair again.

Bedford Heights plumbing questions

No. Bedford Heights is a residential area inside the City of Bedford in Tarrant County. Its schools are part of the Hurst-Euless-Bedford school district.

Bedford Heights Elementary and Bedford Junior High both list 76021 addresses. Other parts of Bedford use 76022, so confirm your own ZIP code on a bill or a mailing.

Search your address on the Tarrant Appraisal District site. The property record lists the year built, which helps you judge how old your water heater and fixtures may be.

The City of Bedford posts agendas and project lists on its website, and Public Works answers questions about the water system. Ask the city before you dig or schedule a large repair near the street.

Give your street address and the nearest cross street. Bedford Heights has no documented boundary, so a street address is clearer than a neighborhood name.

Meadow Park is a name Bedford uses for an area of the city. The Meadow Park Athletic Complex is at 3200 Meadow Park Drive, and homes along Meadow Park Drive, Meadow Place and Meadow Court are recorded in the Bedford Meadows Addition. If your address uses one of those street names, this guide fits you.

Two names for one general area

Residents may say Meadow Park, while county plat records use the Bedford Meadows Addition. The City of Bedford's 2024 long-range plan also uses Meadow Park for a land use category, which it renamed Neighborhood Village. None of these sources draws an exact boundary that we could confirm.

Because the boundary is uncertain, we describe the area by its anchor streets. That is more honest than claiming a neighborhood line, and it is also what a plumber needs to find the right door.

Hard water is the everyday issue here

This page leans on the one local fact that applies to every Bedford kitchen and bath. The city's 2025 Water Quality Report lists total hardness of 110 ppm, which the USGS scale calls moderately hard. That is enough to leave scale on aerators, shower heads and the bottom of a tank water heater.

Simple upkeep you can do

  • Soak aerators and shower heads in vinegar.
  • Flush the water heater tank each year.
  • Wipe scale before it hardens.
  • Test your tap before buying a softener.

Questions for a plumber

  • Is my tank due for a flush?
  • Is the anode rod worn?
  • Do I need a softener at 110 ppm?
  • Which fixtures are worth replacing?

A softener is a household choice, and some families are happy without one. If you do want one, water softener installation starts with your actual tap test and household size. Scale on a tank unit also affects the cost to repair a water heater.

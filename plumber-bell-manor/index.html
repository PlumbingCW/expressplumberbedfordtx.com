Bell Manor is a residential area of Bedford, Texas with 76022 addresses. Bell Manor Elementary sits at 1300 Winchester Way, and the neighboring Forest Ridge name appears in the same city records. Forest Ridge Drive is also where City Hall and Generations Park at Boys Ranch are located.

Who to call: the house or the city?

The most useful plumbing question in a neighborhood with active city projects is where the problem starts. Plumbing inside the house and on your side of the meter is the homeowner's to fix. The water main in the street and the sewer main in the street belong to the city.

Check the meter

Turn off every tap, then watch the meter dial. If it moves, water is leaking on your side.

Look at the street

Water bubbling in the street or a wet strip along the curb points to a city line. Report it to the city.

Check every drain

If every drain is slow at once, the cause may be the house line or the main. Call and describe the pattern.

Make the call

Give the address, what you saw and what you tried. That decides the next step.

What the city lists for Bell Manor

The City of Bedford's 2022 street improvement materials list a Bell Manor 1 to 5 and Forest Ridge No. 2 water and sewer item on the city's active projects map. The same list names a Forest Ridge Addition with Boston, Winchester and Knoxville streets. We do not know the current status of that work, and the city is the right source for it.

City utility work does not replace the plumbing inside your house. It does matter when you plan a larger job. Ask the city whether work is scheduled on your street before you pay to replace a service line, because timing can change the cost and the order of the work.

Services people ask about in this area

Water line problems

A drop in pressure or a wet patch in the yard can mean a leak on the line from the meter to the house. See water line repair.

Leaks you cannot see

A rising bill with no wet spot is the usual sign. Leak detection finds the source without opening walls first.

Sewer line trouble

Gurgling in several drains or a smell outdoors can mean a main line stoppage. See sewer line repair.

Water heaters

A tank that rumbles, leaks at the base or stops heating needs a look. See water heater repair.

Water quality is a city-wide matter, so any Bell Manor mineral buildup traces back to the 110 ppm total hardness in the 2025 city report. Scale on aerators and showerheads is routine upkeep and not a sign that a line is failing.

Emergency faucet repair in Bedford, TX handles faucets that cannot be shut off, leak under the sink, spray from the handle or drip nonstop. A drip is not just annoying. A single dripping faucet can waste hundreds of gallons in a year, and a leak in the cabinet can rot wood and grow mold.

Most faucet repairs are quick. A worn cartridge, a bad O-ring or a failed seat accounts for many drips. Others need a new faucet, especially when the finish is corroded or the parts are no longer available.

Faucet will not shut off: what to do first

If the faucet cannot be stopped, or water is spraying under the sink, the quickest fix is the shutoff valve. If that fails too, the main valve is next.

Follow these steps.

Turn the angle stops

The small valves under the sink turn clockwise to close. Turn both hot and cold.

Use the main if they fail

Old stops can seize or leak. Shut the main water valve and open a low faucet to drain.

Dry the cabinet

Remove items from the cabinet, lay towels and place a bucket under a drip.

Photograph the damage

Note stained wood or flooring for insurance.

Common faucet problems

Steady drip from the spout

A worn cartridge, washer or seat is the usual cause.

Leak at the handle

A bad O-ring or loose packing nut lets water escape around the stem.

Low flow

Mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge reduces water flow.

Leak under the sink

Supply lines, shutoff valves or drain connections can fail, not just the faucet.

Repair or replace

If the faucet is a good brand and the parts are available, repair is often the better value. If the finish is flaking, the body is corroded or the same faucet has failed repeatedly, replacement may be better. We also install new faucets and supply lines on request.

Bedford's water has total hardness of 110 ppm in the city's 2025 report, which falls in the moderately hard range. Scale builds in aerators and cartridges over time, and cleaning or replacing them helps restore flow.

Shutoff valves

Old angle stops under sinks often seize. Replacing them while the plumber is there makes future repairs easy.

Scale

Moderately hard water leaves deposits on aerators and fixtures, which affect flow and finish.

Cabinet damage

A slow leak in a sink cabinet can go unseen for months, so check the base of the cabinet for stains.

Outdoor spigots

Hose bibs and exterior faucets can freeze in hard cold snaps, so cover them before a freeze.

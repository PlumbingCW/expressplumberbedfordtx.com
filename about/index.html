Bedford Express Plumbing answers plumbing emergencies in Bedford, Texas and neighboring Hurst. The office is at 1220 Airport Fwy, Bedford, TX 76022, and the phone line is answered around the clock.

We keep the process simple. You call, describe the problem and get told what to do while a plumber is on the way. On arrival the plumber diagnoses the problem and gives you a clear price before any work begins.

What we do

We repair and replace water heaters, clear drains and sewer lines, find hidden leaks and repair burst pipes. We install fixtures and appliances, replace worn supply lines and work on commercial properties. The full list is on our services page.

How to check any plumber in Texas

Plumbing in Texas is regulated by the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners. Before you hire anyone, ask for the license number and look it up on the board's website. Check the name, the license type and whether it is current. It takes a minute and applies to every plumber, including us.

Ask whether the plumber carries insurance and ask for a written price before work starts. A trustworthy company is comfortable with all three requests.

How we handle pricing

You get a clear price before work starts. We do not quote over the phone for jobs we have not seen, because a leak and a failed sewer line can sound alike on a call. If the price changes, we explain why before continuing.

Where we work

Our service area is Bedford and Hurst. If you are not sure whether we cover your street, call and ask.

What happens when you call

Answer

A person picks up the 24/7 line. You describe the problem and give your address.

Safety advice

The dispatcher tells you what to do first, such as closing the main valve or leaving the house if you smell gas.

Dispatch

A plumber is sent to your address. You are told what to expect.

Diagnosis and price

The plumber finds the cause and gives you a clear price before any work starts.

We book by phone because plumbing problems are hard to judge from a form. A short conversation tells us whether you need an emergency visit or a scheduled one, and it lets us ask about the details that change the job.

What we will not do

We do not promise an arrival time we cannot keep, and we do not quote a firm price for a job nobody has seen. We do not ask for the full amount in cash before work begins. We also do not tell you a repair is needed when a cheaper fix will do. If a part is cheap to replace, we say so.

If a problem is outside what we do, such as a gas utility leak or a city sewer main in the street, we tell you who to call instead of guessing.

Plumbing in Bedford, in context

A 2025 KERA News profile reports that Bedford had about 2,700 residents in 1960 and has around 50,000 today. A large share of the city's homes were built during that growth, so many have older water heaters, supply lines and drain lines. That is the daily reality of our work.

The City of Bedford's 2025 Water Quality Report adds local detail. Water comes from the Trinity River Authority's Tarrant County Water Supply Project and Trinity Aquifer wells, total hardness is 110 ppm and the disinfectant is chloramine. We use those figures, with their source, when we explain scale in water heaters or advise on softeners.

How to get the most from a visit

Before the plumber arrives

  • Clear access to the problem area, such as the water heater closet or the sink cabinet.
  • Know where the main water shutoff is and, if you can, close it.
  • Write down when the problem started and what you have already tried.
  • Photograph water damage for your records and your insurer.
  • Secure pets and keep children away from the work area.

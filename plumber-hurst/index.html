Hurst sits in Tarrant County in the Mid-Cities region of the Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex. It borders Bedford, Euless, North Richland Hills and Richland Hills. The city counted 40,413 residents in the 2020 census, up from 10,165 in 1960, so most of its housing grew in the decades after that.

Before we arrive in Hurst: what to do first

The first few minutes after a plumbing failure matter most. Stop the water or the fuel, protect what you can and keep the line free so the plumber can reach you.

These steps apply to most Hurst emergencies.

Shut the main water valve

Turn the main shutoff clockwise. Look near the water heater, in a garage or at the meter box by the street.

Turn off the water heater

Switch off the breaker for an electric unit or set a gas control to off if the tank is leaking.

Stop using drains

If a drain is backing up, do not run the dishwasher, washer or showers until the line is cleared.

Smell gas? Leave

If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 or your gas utility from outside, then call us.
